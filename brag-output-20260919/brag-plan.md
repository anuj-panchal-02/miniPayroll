# Brag Plan: miniPayroll

## What is this app?
miniPayroll is a payroll application for small companies in India (1-50 employees) that focuses purely on running the monthly payroll, without the bloat of an HR suite.

## The angle
The product's strength is its simplicity and narrow focus. The video will mock the complexity of modern HR suites and highlight how fast and clean it is to just enter attendance and get payslips. The claim is "Payroll suites got too large. This product only runs the month."

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The phrase "Payroll suites got too large" centered on screen. "too large" is highlighted or styled as a landing mark.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A quick visual of "HR, leave, tax, and modules nobody opens" fading or being crossed out.
- The 4-step process cards appearing one by one: 1. Add employee, 2. Monthly attendance, 3. Review & lock, 4. Payslips.
- The Monthly inputs table sliding in, showing a clean grid.

## Outro / punchline
"Payroll without the suite. miniPayroll." with the brand logo.

## User flow worth showing
Add employee → Enter monthly attendance → Review & lock. This sequence will be shown abstractly through the step cards and the monthly input table UI.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: smooth, minimal SaaS presentation focusing on cutting the bloat.
- Interpretation: Pacing is clean and unhurried. Feature cards reveal smoothly. No chaotic motion, just professional restraint.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: oklch(99% 0.005 260)
- Accent: oklch(62% 0.2 150)
- Text: oklch(18% 0.01 260)
- Display font: Geist Sans
- Body font: Geist Sans
- Strongest visual element: The clean, minimal typography and the 4-step showcase layout.

## Share copy (draft)
Payroll suites got too large, so we built miniPayroll—just attendance in and payslips out for small companies.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: upbeat and professional, minimal SaaS mood
- Music treatment: start at 0s, fade out smoothly at 18s
- Music cue guidance: 1-3 strong cues for the feature card reveals; beat-grid window for the 4 steps appearing.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS gently pulses the UI cards' shadow.
- SFX posture: sparse; motion-matched to cards sliding and text revealing.
- Audio-coupled moments: The 4-step cards appear sequentially, each coupled with a clean "pop" or "tick" sound on the beat.
- Restraint rule: No heavy whooshes or aggressive impact sounds. Keep it clean.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook — 4s
Text "Payroll suites got too large" centered. "too large" highlights in green accent color.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: establish the clean, serious-but-approachable tone.
Audio-coupled idea: type the hook line or fade it in cleanly with a subtle tick.
Music: upbeat SaaS mood.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Bloat — 3s
Text "HR, leave, tax, and modules nobody opens." appears, then gets struck through or fades out, replaced by "Attendance in. Figures out. Payslips in hand."
Sequential/interaction: yes — strike through the first text, then reveal the second.
Audio intent: a subtle 'woosh' or crossing-out sound for the strike-through.
Audio-coupled idea: beat-aligned text replacement.
Transition mood: smooth slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The Process — 7s
The phrase "The month, in four steps" at the top. Below it, 4 feature cards slide in sequentially: 1. People and pay, 2. Monthly attendance, 3. Calculate & lock, 4. Payslips.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 cards arrive one by one.
Audio intent: clean, satisfying progression.
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card sequence aligned to beats, with a soft click for each.
Transition mood: smooth wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The Outro — 4s
"Payroll without the suite."
The miniPayroll brand logo appears below it.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: final landing beat.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Transition mood: fade to black
